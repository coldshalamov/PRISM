## Submission and deadline

The organizer requires a working demo, **public** repository, and short pitch deck. Teams have 1–5 participants and submit once in one track. Code must be written during the October 17 **9:00 a.m.–6:00 p.m. EDT** building window; earlier ideas/research/design planning are allowed. Distinguish the supplied preexisting kit and permitted open-source dependencies from new event-day code. The event ends at 8:00 p.m.; that does not extend the **6:00 p.m. submission cutoff**. Finalists pitch for three minutes. [Organizer event listing](https://luma.com/aihackathon26)

The submission page collects track, project name, description, demo/repository/deck URLs, comma-separated stack, and screenshots. Images accept PNG/JPG/GIF/WEBP up to 5 MiB each. Saving a draft is separate from submitting. A team association is required; a solo entrant still needs a team record. [Submission page](https://hackathon.miamiaihub.com/submit)
